biological psychology - it explains biological behavior due to our own psychology.



personality psychology - what makes people unique and gives them their character



social psychology - how we react to other people and how it affects us.

clinical psychology - it is the treatment of behavioral disorders and finds ways to promote psychological health.



health psychology - the role that psychological factors play in physical health and illness.



educational psychology - it is about how students learn and finding out which teaching method is the most effective and which method is useful for which age group.

sports psychology - it examines the psychological factors that affect performance and participation in sports and exercise.



community psychology - focuses on how individuals are connected to and part of their communities.



forensic psychology - a field that blends psychology, law, and criminal justice.

History of psychology has two perspectives - Science and Experience



Origins of psychology based on experience :-



1\. Prehistoric

&#x20;

&#x20;  Shamans existed who were considered to know it all - they convinced them they had supernatural powers.

&#x20;  They performed trephination - drilling a small hole in a person's skull to release spirits and demons.



2\. Ancient



&#x20;  They slowly moved away from supernatural explanations

&#x20;  Chinese people identified parts of the body of the mind/soul (e.g.: Spleen - ideas and intelligence; Kidneys - will and vitality).

&#x20;  Hippocrates - temperaments associated with bodily fluids.

&#x20;  Socrates - mind and body are different.

&#x20;  Plato - body's humors are connected to emotions.

&#x20;  Aristotle - research of the soul; psychology as a part



3\. Medieval to Early Modern

&#x20;

&#x20;  They believed in supernatural causes.

&#x20;  They built Asylums throughout Europe.

&#x20;  Movements for moral treatment emerged in Europe and United States.

&#x20;  1700-1800 more moral and humane treatments came to existence.

&#x20;  Phillipe Pinel - French Physician and considered the father of modern psychiatry; "moral therapy".



4\. Modern Times



&#x20;  End of 1800s "medical model"

&#x20;  Mental illness were seen as a category of illnesses that needed treatment like physical health conditions

&#x20;  Emil Kraeplin - collected large data to organize and categorize mental illness he also laid the foundation for the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual (DSM)

&#x20;  Schizophrenia (split mind); Depression; Bipolar Disorder

&#x20;  In 1900s - Sigmund Freud

&#x20;  He invented psychoanalysis

&#x20;  Impact of childhood on adulthood



Origin of psychology based on science :-



1\. Empiricism



&#x20;  It is a method of enquiry invented by John Locke.

&#x20;  It is called Tabula Rasa (blank slate) - knowledge and thoughts come from experience - basis for empiricism.



2\. Psychophysics            ***(This will come for the Quiz 1 test)***



&#x20;  They study human perception of light and sound.

&#x20;

&#x20;  It was the first scientific form of psychology.



&#x20;  Lab experiments were conducted subjective of physical sensations.



&#x20;  Relationship between the physical and psychological worlds.
